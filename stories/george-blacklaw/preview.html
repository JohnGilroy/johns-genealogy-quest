George Blacklaw

The Two Lives of George Blacklaw

Illustrated biography · PDF · Landscape format · Published 2026

George Blacklaw first appeared in the family history as an ordinary nineteenth-century agricultural labourer. Yet the surviving records refused to fit together. He seemed to have no childhood, no clear birth record and no trace of a life before he emerged in Midlothian beside Caroline Mathieson.

Farther north, another man with almost the same name, age, occupation and birthplace had vanished from Kincardineshire, leaving behind a wife who called herself a widow although no death could be found. This illustrated biography follows the clues through George’s relationships with Catherine Strachan, Caroline Mathieson and Marion Kerr, and asks whether two apparently separate lives were really one.
