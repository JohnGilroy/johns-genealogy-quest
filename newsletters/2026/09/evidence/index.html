Military Service Evidence Packages

Supporting PDF evidence packages for Newsletter Issue 9, August 2026. Each package gathers surviving military record images with a short synopsis and chronology where available.

  • James Stretton - British Army service record evidence package
    Discharge papers, service calculation and Waterloo reckonable-service credit.
  • Peter Penman - Military record synopsis
    Service in the 42nd Regiment of Foot, Gibraltar, medical discharge and physical description.
  • Joseph Hinshelwood - British Army service record evidence package
    Royal Scots service, South Africa, desertion and return to military authority.
  • Joseph Hinshelwood - Imperial Yeomanry service synopsis
    Later Scottish Horse / Imperial Yeomanry service in South Africa.
  • Joseph Wilson - British Army WWI service record evidence package
    Royal Scots service, France, Salonica, malaria and demobilisation records.
  • William Bowman Seton - Official record synopsis
    Bombay birth and baptism context, Addiscombe nomination papers and Bombay Military Fund register evidence.
